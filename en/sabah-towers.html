The Project

Space, light
and wellbeing

Sabah Towers is designed for those who want a calm resort lifestyle by the sea without disconnecting from the city. According to the developer, it is the first project in Azerbaijan with a Longevity Wellness concept: personal renewal and a balance between an active life and tranquillity.

According to the catalogue, the project has five residential buildings and an additional hotel-concept building, 864 apartments and 3.6-metre ceilings. The first and second floors include retail space.
