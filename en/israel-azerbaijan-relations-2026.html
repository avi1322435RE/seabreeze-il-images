Israel–Azerbaijan Relations: The Partnership Between Two Countries

History, diplomacy, trade and what it means for travellers and investors

OHAYON PROPERTIES | BAKU · Updated November 2026

Introduction: ties that go beyond geographic distance

Israel and Azerbaijan differ in history, language and culture, yet since the early 1990s they have built a broad political and economic relationship. For visitors to Baku or anyone considering business in the country, understanding these relations helps explain the background to trade, tourism and investment ties. The ties do not erase legal, political or business differences between the countries. In fact, the distinction between political friendship and the review of an individual transaction is especially important for readers interested in real estate in Azerbaijan.

1991–1993: the start of diplomatic relations

Israel recognised Azerbaijan's independence on 25 December 1991. Diplomatic relations were established on 7 April 1992, after the dissolution of the Soviet Union. In August 1993 the Israeli embassy opened in Baku.

These steps laid the foundation for ongoing political dialogue and for ties between companies, institutions and citizens. In the following years the areas of cooperation expanded and official representatives made reciprocal visits.

2023: Azerbaijan opens its embassy in Israel

In March 2023 Azerbaijan's embassy in Israel opened, with the official ceremony held on 29 March. The foreign ministers of both countries and other representatives attended. It was a symbolic and practical milestone, as it allows a permanent Azerbaijani diplomatic presence in Israel.

According to the Azerbaijani Foreign Ministry's statement on the event, the two sides pointed to areas of cooperation such as the economy, energy, tourism, education and security. However, the event in itself says nothing about the volume of future investments or deals.

Energy, trade and investment: the economic foundation

Energy is a central pillar of the economic relationship. Azerbaijan produces oil and gas, and Israel is a consumer and technology market with commercial ties to the country. Alongside energy, official documents mention agriculture, information technology, cybersecurity, pharmaceuticals, logistics and tourism.

The Azerbaijani Foreign Ministry publishes export figures from Azerbaijan to Israel for previous years, but the figures vary from year to year. An old trade figure should therefore not be presented as if it reflects current trade volumes. For an up-to-date business review, use official annual trade data and state clearly the period examined.

In 2017 a joint intergovernmental commission for cooperation was established. Mechanisms of this kind are designed to promote ties between government bodies and the business sector, but they are not a commitment to commercial outcomes.

Innovation, agriculture and technology

Israel is known for its work in precision agriculture, water, health and technology. The Azerbaijani Foreign Ministry notes the involvement of Israeli companies in fields including agriculture, cyber and information technology.

For Azerbaijan, such cooperation may fit its aim of diversifying the economy beyond its dependence on energy. However, the actual scope of activity should be assessed through contracts, reports and specific data, not only political statements.

The human connection: Jewish communities and people-to-people ties

Azerbaijan has a long history of Jewish life. The Jewish communities in the country and the Azerbaijani community in Israel are mentioned in diplomatic documents as a human and cultural bridge between the countries.

People-to-people ties are reflected in family visits, cultural exchange, tourism and business meetings. Alongside the positive story, it is important to present Azerbaijani society in its complexity and not to claim that every person or institution in the country holds the same view of Israel.

2025–2026: the official dialogue continues

In February 2025 the foreign ministers of Israel and Azerbaijan met on the sidelines of the Munich Security Conference and discussed, among other things, energy, investment, technology and security. In August 2025 the Azerbaijani Foreign Ministry reported a meeting with Israel's new ambassador, Ronen Krausz.

On 26 January 2026 the foreign ministers met in Baku. The official statement highlighted the potential for cooperation in energy, trade, investment, tourism, logistics, technology and health. This is a report on an agenda and talks, not proof that specific projects are being carried out.

What does this mean for travellers?

Diplomatic ties provide official communication channels and consular services, but they do not replace preparing for a trip. Before flying, check visa requirements, passport validity, travel advisories, insurance and current entry rules. Even when governments have good relations, entry conditions and the security situation can change. It is best to rely on your own foreign ministry and on Azerbaijan's official migration authorities rather than on old marketing material.

And what does it mean for a property investor?

Diplomatic and economic relations may make it easier to build business connections, but they do not guarantee returns, appreciation or special protection for a buyer.

Before buying, verify the seller's identity, the registration of rights, the type of property, the permit status, payment terms, maintenance costs, tax obligations and the rules for transferring funds. In joint purchases, the relationship between the partners should also be set out in writing.

An investor should also examine reporting and tax obligations in their home country. The existence of agreements between states does not, without a specific review, mean that there is no tax, no double taxation, or that funds can be transferred without restrictions.

Summary: a developing partnership, independent checks

More than three decades after relations were established, Israel and Azerbaijan maintain political dialogue and ties across a variety of economic and technological fields. The opening of the Azerbaijani embassy in Israel in 2023 and the working meetings in the years that followed are notable milestones.

For readers of OHAYON PROPERTIES | BAKU, this context matters for understanding the country and its business environment. Still, a good investment decision rests on checking the property, the data and the documents – not on relations between states alone.

Frequently asked questions

When did diplomatic relations begin?

On 7 April 1992.

When did Azerbaijan's embassy in Israel open?

The embassy began operating in March 2023; the official opening ceremony was held on 29 March 2023.

In which fields do the countries cooperate?

According to official statements: energy, trade, investment, agriculture, technology, tourism and other fields.

Do good relations guarantee that investing in Baku real estate is worthwhile?

No. Every transaction requires a review of viability, rights, costs, tax and risks.
