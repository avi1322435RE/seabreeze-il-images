The Project

Inspired by the East,
a home for the family

Arabian Ranches Baku is located in the Sea Breeze district and draws inspiration from Eastern architecture. According to the developers, the project offers apartments and townhouses designed around family life, with resort-style amenities.

The renderings show arches, sand-toned facades, balconies with wooden screens and green spaces between the buildings.
